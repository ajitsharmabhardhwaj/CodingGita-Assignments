Symbol Reference

Reserved characters: < represents less than, > represents greater than, and & represents an ampersand.

Currency symbols: € for Euro, £ for Pound, and ₹ for Indian Rupee.

Mathematical symbols: ≠ means not equal to, ∑ represents summation, and ∞ represents infinity.

Arrows: ← indicates left, → indicates right, and ↑ indicates upward direction.

Symbols: 😊 shows a smiling face emoji, © indicates copyright, and ® indicates a registered trademark.
